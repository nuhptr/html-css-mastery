Frequently Asked Questions

01 Where are these chairs assembled?

Every chair frame is crafted from FSC-certified European beechwood and assembled by hand in our Lisbon workshop before rigorous quality inspection.

  • Kiln-dried solid timber joinery for zero creaking.
  • Water-based matte finishes safe for indoor living spaces.
  • Individually load-tested prior to packaging.
02 How long do I have to return my chair?

Try your chair at home for up to 30 days. If it does not fit your space or posture, schedule a complimentary courier pickup for a full refund.

  • 30-day risk-free home trial from delivery date.
  • Free return shipping within the EU & UK.
  • 5-year structural frame warranty included.
03 Do you ship to countries outside the EU?

Yes — we ship worldwide via climate-neutral freight partners to North America, Japan, Singapore, and Australia with tracked doorstep delivery.

  • International transit averages 7–12 business days.
  • Flat-rate protective crating for multi-chair orders.
  • Customs duties calculated transparently at checkout.
