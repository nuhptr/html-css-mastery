Relative Units: em vs. rem

Parent-Relative em (Compounds in Nested Lists)

This container sets font-size: 20px , so 1.2em on this paragraph computes to 24px .

  • Level 1 item (1.2em = 24px)
  • Nested list compounds:
    • Level 2 item (1.2em × 1.2em = 28.8px)
    • Level 2 item

Root-Relative rem (Consistent Scale)

Using rem always references the root <html> font size (typically 16px ), preventing compounding surprises in nested components.
